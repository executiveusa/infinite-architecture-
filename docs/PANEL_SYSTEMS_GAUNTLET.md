# Panel Systems Field Note — release gauntlet

## Mode
Brownfield. Preserve Infinite Architecture routes, tokens, provenance language, safety boundaries, dashboard behavior and technical field-mode architecture.

## Outcome
A first-time visitor can understand the lightweight panel concept, choose EPS/XPS/cement board/fiber cement, understand flexible vs rigid adhesive roles, see plausible concept uses, and reach manufacturer evidence without reading a manual.

## Quality bar
Visual/craft reference: the live COLLINS website at https://www.wearecollins.com/ — compare real screenshots at equivalent desktop/mobile viewports. Do not imitate its identity; compare hierarchy, editorial pacing, surprise, clarity, interaction and craft.

## ADHD gate
Lead with one obvious interaction. Recognition over recall. Progressive disclosure. Plain language. Maximum five peer choices at once. Hide technical depth behind taps/accordions. Preserve state. No dead controls. Mobile tap targets >= 44px.

## Independent critics
1. Customer-value critic: can a visitor explain the system and why it matters after one screen?
2. Krug usability critic: self-evidence, navigation, recovery, mobile and cognitive load.
3. Creative/taste critic: distinctive Infinite Architecture identity, typography, rhythm, imagery, motion and anti-template quality.
4. Accessibility critic: keyboard, focus, semantics, contrast, reduced motion and responsive behavior.
5. Technical/safety critic: truthful claims, source provenance, compatibility caveats, performance, broken links and runtime states.
6. Sovereignty critic: no new lock-in; owner retains code/data/assets; rollback is documented.

## Loop
Break the experience into independently judgeable pieces: entry/hero, core chooser, assembly explorer, concept-use gallery, adhesive library, evidence/safety. For each piece, use a builder and a separate critic with fresh context. Compare ours blind against the reference where comparable. The critic names the single biggest remaining gap. Iterate until ours wins the blind comparison or owner stops the run.

## Release gate
Overall, usability, visual, originality and accessibility >= 8.5/10; comprehension/primary interaction >= 9/10; zero critical failures; zero broken controls; zero mobile overflow; zero unverified claims presented as fact; keyboard and reduced-motion coverage; rollback documented; owner approval recorded.

## Current evidence state
The new panel-systems route is a bounded first slice. It is not production-verified until lint, typecheck, build, browser/mobile interaction, source-link verification and independent critic passes are recorded.
