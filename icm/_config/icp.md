# Ideal Customer Profile

Primary customer: an owner or operator who already owns, controls, or is seriously evaluating a
property near Puerto Vallarta, Bahía de Banderas, Sayulita, San Pancho, or nearby Riviera Nayarit.
They intend to create or improve roughly 2–15 distinctive nature-based hospitality units.

Qualifying signals: property control, commercial intent, plausible investment capacity, a decision
timeline, and willingness to pay for clarity before procurement or construction.

Do not optimize the homepage for generic inspiration seekers, DIY builders, or vendors.
