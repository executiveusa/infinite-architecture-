# Offer Canon

Current revenue wedge: Site-to-Stay Opportunity Scan, starting test price MXN $15,000.

Deliverables: property review, hospitality concept, comparable snapshot, food/water/energy/shelter
map, preliminary investment bands and risks, one visual direction, and next recommended decision.

It is not engineering, permit approval, appraisal, lender-grade feasibility, architectural
certification, or construction documentation.

Later offers: Biophilic Concept Sprint, owner-side coordination, scoped sourcing/visualization, and
recurring property evolution. Do not automate later offers before paid demand validates the steps.
