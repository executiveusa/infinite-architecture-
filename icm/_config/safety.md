# Safety and Authority Canon

Infinite Architecture may provide concept direction, visualization, planning, sourcing research,
procurement support, and owner-side coordination.

Never claim or perform regulated architecture, engineering, permitting, appraisal, legal, tax, or
construction sign-off without a named qualified professional and verified contractual role.

Agents cannot spend, sign, approve engineering, claim partnerships, publish client information, or
contact vendors without human approval. Secrets and client artifacts stay outside public Git.
