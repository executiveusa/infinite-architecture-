# 01 Demand

**Inputs:** ICP, offer, approved claims, channel, and campaign hypothesis.

**Process:** Select qualified local prospects and referral paths; publish one useful property
decision at a time; route one CTA to the Opportunity Scan.

**Outputs:** Prospect list, sourced content, outreach draft, and attributable campaign ID.

**Verify:** Correct ICP, no fake proof, one CTA, and human approval before direct outreach.
