# 02 Qualification

**Inputs:** Supabase inquiry record and any client-supplied links.

**Process:** Confirm property control, intended use, investment range, timeline, location, and main
uncertainty. Identify missing information without giving regulated conclusions.

**Outputs:** Fit decision, follow-up questions, next action, and status update.

**Verify:** Consent exists, record stays private, and no proposal is sent without human review.
