# 03 Opportunity Scan

**Inputs:** Paid scope, property evidence, customer outcome, local constraints, and approved research.

**Process:** Review opportunity, guest concept, comparables, living systems, investment bands,
sourcing paths, risks, visual direction, and next decision.

**Outputs:** Bounded scan with assumptions, exclusions, sources, confidence, and decision gate.

**Verify:** Payment/scope confirmed; no engineering or permit claim; human approval before delivery.
