# 05 Sourcing

**Inputs:** Approved requirements, evidence standard, target location, and budget basis.

**Process:** Research alternatives, request comparable RFQs after approval, calculate landed cost,
and expose warranties, exclusions, lead times, local labor, and dependencies.

**Outputs:** Evidence-backed comparison and recommended human decision.

**Verify:** No unverified partnership, hidden markup, autonomous contact, commitment, or spending.
