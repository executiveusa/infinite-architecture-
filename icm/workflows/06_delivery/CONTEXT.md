# 06 Delivery

**Inputs:** Signed scopes, named responsible parties, approved concept, schedule, and dependencies.

**Process:** Track decisions, handoffs, procurement status, design intent, risks, and evidence while
qualified parties own their regulated deliverables.

**Outputs:** Current decision log, responsibility map, progress record, and next human gate.

**Verify:** Contracts and insurance reviewed; owner control preserved; rollback/recovery documented.
