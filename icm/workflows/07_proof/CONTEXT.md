# 07 Proof

**Inputs:** Approved project evidence, permissions, measurements, sources, and final outcomes.

**Process:** Separate built, proposed, research, and inspiration; describe assumptions and what was
actually verified; remove client/private information.

**Outputs:** Case study, Field Note, reusable campaign assets, and evidence record.

**Verify:** Client permission, rights, claim support, safety review, and human publishing approval.
